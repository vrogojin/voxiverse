**Two fixes 2026-07-31 (deploy/cheats-eyeball, live; gate-verified; not visually eyeballed — see notes).**

**FLIGHT-LANDING FALL-THROUGH — `FP_DESCENT_FACET_RESYNC` (ddf35e0, verify_descent_facet_resync 9/0, FLAT 6042/0).** Distinct from the dev-teleport fall-through ([[voxiverse-fallthrough-loc-bug]] FP_TP_FLOOR_WELD): this hits NORMAL flight-descent at FAR spots. Root cause: the planet is piecewise-flat (each facet = a flat tangent plane); during fast/high flight over a far region the ACTIVE facet LAGS the true sub-camera facet (seam crossings cooldown/containment-deferred in maybe_cross_facet + the high-flyer pool freeze _pool_off_surface suppresses re-designation). The player's `position` then sits many facets past the stale facet's centre IN THAT FACET'S FRAME, where the flat datum plane extended past its ridge DIVES BELOW the sphere → `surface_y`/`floor_under`/`effective_height` (world_manager.gd:3599, keyed on TerrainConfig.active_facet()) return a DEEP value (measured −28 to −100) while the world radius stays ≈R. Live symptom: flew far, fell to alt −20; `set_alt 8`→−20 (surface_y≈−28). Fix: `resync_subcamera_facet(pos)` (world_manager.gd:2434) resolves the true `facet_of_dir` owner and — ONLY when it's drifted ≥2 facets (NON-ADJACENT, unreachable by the adjacent-only seam march ⇒ unambiguously a stale-facet desync, never a walk step) — redesignates onto it via `_commit_facet_change` (position/velocity-continuous). Wired in player.gd:772 FACETED branch when `not flying and not _dev_nav`, before maybe_cross_facet. Fixes surface_y/floor_under/streaming at the ROOT (not just a floor clamp). Adjacent flips stay with maybe_cross_facet hysteresis → normal walking untouched. NOTE: NOT live-eyeballed — reproducing needs fast flight to a far spot (remote thrust is walk-speed only, can't drive it); gate-proven.

**DIM SUNRISE/SUNSET SUN DISC — `FP_SUN_DISC_WARM` (679b4ef, verify_atmo_sky +G-DIMSUN 10 new pass, FLAT 6042/0).** The sun DISC's atmospheric extinction was applied TWICE in cosmos_sky.gd `_update_sky` (FP_SUN_PRESENCE+FP_SUN_PATHLIGHT stack): once as transmittance colour `reddened=_SUN_EMISSION_BASE*path_transmittance(m)` (:1188) AND again as luminance energy `8*path_luminance(m)*occ` (:1201). At the horizon (m≈18) this stacks to ~m² extinction → disc collapses to ~0.29 luminance (near-black dot) exactly when a sunrise/sunset sun should be big+warm. Fix: renormalize the disc transmittance to a chroma-preserving HUE (keep warm R≥G≥B reddening, drop the luminance collapse) + drop the L(m) double-extinction from disc emission + glare. Result: bright warm-orange disc at horizon (~12×), noon unchanged. Occlusion (occ_cam, FP_TERM_SUN_CONT ground-floor) still hides it below the horizon; the DirectionalLight scene illuminance keeps full T⃗·L dimming (untouched). Requires FP_SUN_PRESENCE+FP_SUN_PATHLIGHT on. NOT eyeballed at a live low sun (hard to aim remote camera at the moving disc) — gate-proven; eyeball at real sunrise. Pre-existing verify_atmo_sky H_RIM 5-fail (H_RIM=128 vs gate's 40-64 band) is UNRELATED, unchanged.
