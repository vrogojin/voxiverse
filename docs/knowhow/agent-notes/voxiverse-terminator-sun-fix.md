**Bug** (user, live): on the ground at dawn/dusk, walking — it's bright but the Sun disc is INVISIBLE, then one step and the Sun POPS in a bit over the horizon; brightness/atmosphere/Sun all jump per step.

**Root cause** (Fable, read-only, verdict PRE-EXISTING — NOT O1; O1/FP_ATMO_GROUND_BUDGET only touched the atmo-SHELL brightness, never sun position/occlusion, so the clean rollback build still had it):
- The disc visibility was a BINARY flip `_sun.visible = occ_cam >= 0.5` (cosmos_sky.gd ~1097). The sun-hide angle dep(h)=asin(r_vox/dist)≈√(2h/R) is SINGULAR in eye-height h — infinite slope at the datum, and PINNED at 90° (hard kink) below the datum (oceans/valleys, since plains sit only BASE_HEIGHT≈5±4 above the datum sphere). So a ±1-block walk step jerks the 0.5-crossing threshold by up to a whole disc radius ⇒ the disc pops in/out per step. Disc material is UNSHADED (albedo self-renders at full even at emission 0) so the binary flip was load-bearing.
- "Bright but no Sun" = three deliberately WIDE dawn curves (near-block shade TERMINATOR_MU=0.12, DirectionalLight PEN_GROUND=0.10, ambient ±0.15 twilight) read "day" a good 1–7° before the disc is allowed to appear (disc hidden until el=−dep(h)). The window is defensible dawn; the BUG is its top edge (the disc flip) being binary + datum-referenced + step-jittered.

**Fix** = `FP_TERM_SUN_CONT` (cube_sphere.gd + cosmos_sky.gd only, byte-identical off, FLAT 6042/0, atmo-sky gate 138/0, commit c9245dd on fix/voxiverse-terminator-sun → merged c95a96e on deploy/perf-plus-sky):
1. Continuous presence: disc + Moon fade CONTINUOUSLY by occlusion (albedo·occ on the unshaded impostor; also non-B0 emission) and stay `visible` on a `>0.001` threshold — no boolean flip.
2. Bound the singularity: new static `occlusion_factor_ground` floors the occluder distance at `r_vox + TERM_SUN_H_EYE_MIN` (=2 blocks), capping dep'(h) at ~0.36°/block and removing the below-datum kink; also fed to the absolute light/ambient (`light_energy_ground`/`ambient_ground`) so per-step brightness stops jittering.
3. Gate G-TSC proves BOTH: fix stays <0.15/block AND the shipped binary presence pops ≥0.99 in one step.
- Requires FP_SKY_PLANET_OCCLUDE and/or FP_LIGHT_ABSOLUTE (both already ON in the live deploy). Skipped the optional EMA `_h_smooth` (add only if residual jitter remains). Did NOT touch the wide-dawn tuning (separate knob if brightness still leads the Sun too much).
